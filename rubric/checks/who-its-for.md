Act as an agent working for a prospective customer. Ask: Who is this for? Which industries, roles, company sizes or situations does it serve? Where does it operate (countries, regions, languages)? Are there stated qualifying criteria or things it does not do? Answer from the retrieved page text only, quoting the passages you rely on, and judge how confidently a prospect could tell whether they are a fit.

Scoring:
- 0: The content gives no indication of who the offering is for.
- 1: The audience is implied by tone or examples but never stated.
- 2: The audience is stated in general terms (for example "businesses") without specifics.
- 3: The audience is stated with industries, roles or situations, and location or coverage is clear.
- 4: The audience, coverage and fit criteria are stated clearly, including what the organisation does not do.
