Judge the title element and meta description of the home page and sampled pages. A good title names the page and the organisation and is unique per page; a good description summarises the page in one or two sentences in plain language. Look for missing, duplicated, truncated or keyword-stuffed values, and for descriptions that do not match the page content.

Scoring:
- 0: Titles or descriptions are missing from the sampled pages.
- 1: Titles exist but are duplicated across pages or uninformative; descriptions are missing or boilerplate.
- 2: Titles are unique and reasonable; descriptions are present on some pages.
- 3: Unique, descriptive titles and descriptions on every sampled page, with minor issues.
- 4: Every sampled page has a specific, accurate title and a plain-language description that matches its content.
