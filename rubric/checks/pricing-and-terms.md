Act as an agent working for a prospective customer. Ask: What does it cost? Are there plans, tiers, packages or an engagement model? Is there a free tier, trial or minimum commitment? What are the terms of engagement? Answer from the retrieved page text only, quoting the passages you rely on. Many organisations do not publish prices; in that case judge whether the site at least explains how pricing works and how to get a quote. Do not penalise a stated "contact us for pricing" as heavily as silence.

Scoring:
- 0: Nothing about cost, pricing model or how to obtain a price.
- 1: A vague mention that pricing exists, with no model and no route to a quote.
- 2: The pricing model or engagement model is explained and a route to a quote is given, but no figures.
- 3: Indicative prices, ranges or plan structure are published, with terms summarised.
- 4: Clear published pricing or plans with terms, inclusions and how to proceed.
