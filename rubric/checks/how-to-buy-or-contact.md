Act as an agent working for a prospective customer who wants to proceed. Ask: How do I buy, sign up or start? How do I contact the organisation, and what are the contact details (email, phone, address, booking link)? What happens next and how quickly do they respond? Answer from the retrieved page text and the forms found only, quoting the passages you rely on. Contact details that appear only inside images or scripts, or contact forms with no stated alternative, are hard for an agent to act on.

Scoring:
- 0: No way to buy or make contact can be found in the content.
- 1: A contact form exists but no direct details (email, phone or address) and no explanation of the process.
- 2: Direct contact details or a purchase route exist but are hard to find or incomplete.
- 3: Clear contact details and a clear route to buy or start, easy to find.
- 4: Contact details, purchase or booking routes and what to expect next are stated plainly and are easy to find.
