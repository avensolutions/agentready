Act as an agent working for a prospective customer who has just landed on this site. Form the questions such a person would ask first: What does this organisation do? What products or services does it offer? What problem does it solve and how? Answer each question using only the retrieved page text and structured data, quoting the passages you rely on. Judge how completely and unambiguously the content answers them. Marketing language that never states what is actually offered should score low even if the pages are long.

Scoring:
- 0: The content does not say what the organisation does.
- 1: The offering can be guessed from fragments, but no page states it plainly.
- 2: The offering is stated but vaguely, or the products and services are listed without explaining what they are.
- 3: The offering is stated clearly with its main products or services described.
- 4: The offering, its products or services and the problems they solve are stated plainly on the pages an agent would read first.
