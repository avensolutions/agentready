Whether an agent acting for a prospective customer can answer the questions that person would ask, using only the content the site returns. What does the organisation do, who is it for, what does it cost, and how do you buy or make contact. This is the test that matters most to a business: a site that agents cannot answer from is a site agents will not recommend.
