Whether agents and AI crawlers are allowed to reach the site and can find its content without guesswork. This covers the robots.txt rules that apply to AI user agents, an XML sitemap, and the llms.txt and llms-full.txt conventions that point agents at the pages that matter. If an agent cannot get in or cannot find the right pages, nothing else on the site helps it.
